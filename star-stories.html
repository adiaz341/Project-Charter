Managing Conflicting Priorities in an IT Project

Source: Project Presentation

Prompt (if PMI story): —

Situation

During our semester-long IT portfolio project, our team had overlapping deadlines for requirements gathering, building the website, and preparing a midterm presentation. Several teammates also had exams that week, which caused stress and missed internal deadlines.

Task

As the project coordinator, I needed to keep the project on track, rebalance workload, and make sure we delivered a complete, professional portfolio by the original deadline.

Action

I led a quick working session to list all remaining tasks, estimate effort, and identify bottlenecks. I created a simple Kanban board to visualize priorities, reassigned some testing tasks from the busiest teammates to those with more capacity, and negotiated a small scope reduction on nonessential design elements. I also sent a short status email to our professor outlining what we would deliver by each milestone.

Result

We met the original deadline, delivered all required project artifacts, and received positive feedback on our organization and communication. Our team earned an A on the project, and we continued using the Kanban board for the rest of the semester.

Applying Stakeholder Engagement to a Class Project

Source: PMI Principle 2 – Stewardship / Stakeholder Engagement

Prompt (for PMI stories): Describe a time you applied stakeholder engagement to improve a project outcome.

Situation

In an earlier group project, our team was building a prototype for a campus app, but feedback from our “stakeholders” (classmates and instructor) was limited and inconsistent.

Task

I needed to gather clearer expectations from our stakeholders so we could prioritize features and avoid rework before the final demo.

Action

I scheduled a brief survey and a 10-minute Q&A with the instructor, summarized what we heard into three priority requirements, and shared those with the team. We aligned our backlog to match those priorities and checked in once more before implementation.

Result

The final prototype reflected exactly what the instructor and classmates cared about most, and our team received specific praise for how we incorporated stakeholder input.
